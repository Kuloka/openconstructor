How this page is made

Numbers are copied by hand from the four public pages on the dates shown and are not adjusted. Boards change every few days, so check the source before a big decision. "Preliminary" means Arena has too few votes yet for a stable score. Model names are the makers' own; settings such as "High" or "Max" are folded into one row per model. Something wrong or out of date? Tell us on the changelog page.
