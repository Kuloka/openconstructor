What's new

New features of the free AI interface builder. Open the editor →

November 7, 2026
  • Free AI streams live — the built-in AI (no key) now shows its reasoning, text and code as they are written.
  • AI pictures — let the free FLUX model draw a picture: Ctrl+K → AI picture, or “✦ AI image…” in the block editor.
  • AI models, tested — every free model on Groq and OpenRouter on the same task: speed, format and design, with screenshots and a cost table for cheap paid ones.
  • Phones and tablets — free-layout pages now turn into one column on phones and scale down on tablets — no tiny desktop copy.
  • No more page strips — a site made of full-width sections hugs its content: no band of background above or below.
  • Lock a block — right-click → lock position, so big background blocks don't move by accident.
  • LÖVE export comes alive — buttons react to the mouse and call your handlers, text fields accept typing.
  • AI answers stream live — with your own key the reply appears as it is written — reasoning, text and code, no more staring at a spinner.
  • Edit one part with AI — double-click a block, pick a button or heading and tell the AI what to change: only that part is rewritten.
  • Your own domain — connect mysite.com to a published page; every screen gets its own address like /about.
  • Download a real project — export a static site or a ready Vite + React app as .zip, pictures as files.
  • Ask anything — the chat answers questions on any topic in formatted text, without touching your page.
  • Light editor — switch the editor to a light look in the status bar.
  • Dark / light version — the AI recolors every block of a page for a dark or a light background.
  • Scroll animations — blocks of published pages can appear smoothly while scrolling — pure CSS, no scripts.
  • “Add next” suggestions — after a page is built, one click adds the usual missing sections: pricing, FAQ, testimonials…
  • Brand kit — read colours, font and logo from your website (or set them by hand) — the AI follows them in every block.
  • ✦ Make it better — an AI design review of any block: hierarchy, spacing, contrast and mobile fit, fixed in place.
  • Free AI on open models — GPT-OSS 120B and Llama 3.3 70B on Cloudflare, no key needed.
  • Published pages you control — link-only or in the gallery, turn off, view counts, working forms with messages by e-mail.
  • My components, 3 variants, icons, Ctrl+K — save your own blocks, get three takes on one request, 1,500+ icons and a command palette.
  • Languages — the editor speaks English, Русский, Türkçe, Español and Deutsch.
  • Projects and keys — a list of every project on this device; AI keys remembered in every tab, encrypted on the device; redo.
  • 3D models — ask for a 3D object: it renders live on the canvas and downloads as .glb.
November 6, 2026
  • Publish in one click — a free link for every page, community gallery and remix.
  • 200+ components — web, game and Roblox UI, resizable pages, smart guides and version history.
  • Export anywhere — HTML, React, Vue, Svelte, Roblox (Luau / .rbxmx) and LÖVE 2D.
